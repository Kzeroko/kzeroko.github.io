**Isekai Structures is gone as a separate module.**

It used to own KTM2's towns, dungeons, outposts and ruins. All of that content now lives inside [Isekai Expansion](/en/ktm2/modules/#expansion) itself.

That takes the [Isekai Series](/en/ktm2/modules/) from six modules to five: Core, Expansion, Tweaks, Cuisine, Melody.

## What players need to do

Nothing. There is one fewer jar in the pack and not one piece of content less: structures generate as before, and loot chests, respawn altars and the rest of the structure-side features are all where they were.
