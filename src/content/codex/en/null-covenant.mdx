## What it holds to, and how it works

The Covenant has no public declaration. New members copy out a single sentence:

> When a formula invalidates evidence itself, stop the formula first.

Its targets are the ones ordinary courts cannot try: curses that rewrite a witness's memory, formulae that continuously copy themselves, Star Tower incidents that leave a city holding two histories at once.

Its methods include sealing, evacuation, severing the medium, fabricating a harmless accident report, and — when nothing else is available — executing the caster.

## The contradiction at its centre

Some disasters have to be stopped before the public can understand them. But **a necessary secret with nobody watching it very quickly learns to call dissent a disaster.**

The dangerous part of the Covenant has never been the kill order. It is the organisation gradually treating "skipping the explanation" as efficiency.
