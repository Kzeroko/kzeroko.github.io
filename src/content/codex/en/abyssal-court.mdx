## What it demands

The Court asks for three things:

1. Recognition that demons were not the single guilty party of the Divine Age war;
2. An end to campaigns and expulsions waged by race;
3. Legal standing for Abyss City to trade and to negotiate over land.

Rhavaniel is willing to stop fighting. She refuses to trade unilateral disarmament for a verbal guarantee from human states.

## About the word "Demon Lord"

It is a **wartime command title** raised by the clans, not the throne of an evil ruler of the world. The human translation kept the fear in it; inside the Court the sense is closer to *the one who carries the weight*.

## The contradiction at its centre

Concentrated power kept the exiled alive, and it also made "survival requires it" an endlessly renewable reason to postpone self-government and reconciliation. The Fourfold Hearth Seats are both the source of Rhavaniel's authority and the limit on it — her arguments with them matter far more than any treacherous subordinate ever could.
