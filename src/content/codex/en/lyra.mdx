The elders like to discuss century-long plans. Lyra's job is translating them into tasks someone can carry out tomorrow morning: which day this group of spirits migrates, which route the outside caravans take, how the gathering quota is split, and what exactly her sister's very short order from abroad is supposed to mean.

She knows ordinary grove-hold granaries and grove-hold disputes better than her sister does, which means she also knows best who is working late to fill in the hole behind every "the Queen went herself".

She speaks gently and decides quickly, and her jokes usually hide in the second half of a perfectly straight sentence. What she is best at is making sure everyone in the room has a way to back down. Whether she has one herself is a question very few people have asked.

## How she got here

She and her sister Aethelind were born on the same day in a grove-hold on the forest's edge, and were adopted into the royal main line together as children.

In 859 ST the previous queen died and the Three Seats raised her sister to the throne. Lyra stayed, starting as Inner Court scribe, and spent year after year straightening out the entire administrative apparatus. A great deal of the reason the Moonshadow forest still functions is her work — signed, mostly, in someone else's name.

When her sister leaves the forest, she puts on the crown and governs in her place. The core members of the Three Seats know. Ordinary people only know the Queen has been seen less lately. The sisters look alike, but she has never deceived anyone with magic — she simply put her own handwriting, her own stance and her own habits away, a little at a time.

The arrangement worked for a long time. Until the day she made a decision that was different from her sister's, and better.
