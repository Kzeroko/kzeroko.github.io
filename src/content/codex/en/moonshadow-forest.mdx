| | |

| Nature | Elven heartland, migrating settlements, and the World Tree's root system |
| Population | ~43,000, spread across migrating grove-holds and canopy settlements |
| Government | The [Moonshadow Conclave](/en/codex/moonshadow-conclave/) and the individual grove-holds |
| Chapter | Mid-game region; vertical forest and the World Tree line |

Moonshadow is not a static ancient wood. Elves move homes, roads and gathering grounds on cycles of several decades so that no ley line and no spirit is exhausted by a single use.

The World Tree stands where the forest's real and possible layers meet. Its roots draw water and mana — and hold the traces of history the World Forge cut away.

## Four rings of pacing

- **Outer ring** — you navigate by sound, wind direction and ecological cues.
- **Middle ring** — moving roads and grove-hold daily life.
- **Inner ring** — politics, memory and the vertical canopy.
- **Root realm** — several histories of the same place, layered over one another.

You cannot simply follow a minimap in a straight line. Waymarks are leaf orientation, the rhythm of wind chimes and spirit responses — but every rule is taught first in a safe area and has an item-based alternative, so "the forest is mysterious" never becomes "you are lost with no prompt".
