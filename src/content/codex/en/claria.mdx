She runs the See's inspection work, which means most of her year is spent on the road: border relief this month, checking the seals on a mountain hospital's medicine cabinet the next. People relax the moment they see the white veil at a distance. She knows this perfectly well, and she is very good at using it.

But walk into an actual emergency and you meet a different Claria — sleeves pushed up, jewellery off, triaging while turning every bystander in reach into a pair of hands. At those moments the courtesy disappears entirely and only the shortest instructions are left.

Her smile comes in several versions. The public one is for everybody. Only in private does it reach her eyes first.

## How she got here

She was taken in by the Order of the White Veil as a small child and grew up inside the See.

The Order gave her two things: real skill, learned in border hospitals, and a carefully constructed image as a visible miracle. She did not accept that arrangement passively — she went and learned etiquette, and learned politics, because she worked out early that a saint's reputation converts into things you can hold: medicine, budgets, and one border hospital after another that could not otherwise have been built.

She reads the mortality and refused-admission records every day, and pays particular attention to the cases written up as "arrived too late". She knows what that phrase usually has underneath it — poverty, a blocked road, or an identity check.

In 987 ST, purification began failing in several regions at once. She took charge of the border relief as Chief Inspector, and there invited a stranger of unknown origin to travel with her.
