Meet the actual person and you find her day has nothing to do with conquering anything. She reads four reports first — water, grain, heat wells, casualties — and only then touches military business. She knows the names of most hearth-district supervisors and most front-line squad leaders. She eats the same ration as everyone else, except at diplomatic dinners and victory feasts.

Abyss City is built inside a rift where volcanic ash blocks the sky and almost nothing grows. The demon peoples have been driven here for nine hundred years. Rhavaniel's entire job is making sure this city is still here tomorrow.

She does not raise her voice. When she is genuinely angry she drops every title and every threat and simply states what happens next.

## How she got here

She was born into House Asmodeus, one of the oldest demon bloodlines, inheriting both a dragonblood constitution and a talent for gravity.

In 968 ST the great famine hit Abyss City. The tribes nearly went to war over grain, and it was her compulsory rationing order that stopped it — and saved the city. The following year the tribes raised her to Demon Lord.

The first thing she did in office was not declare war. It was to reorder the supply priority between the heat wells and the hearth districts. She asks the human nations to concede three things: that demons were not the single guilty party of the Divine Age war, that campaigns waged by race must stop, and that Abyss City be granted legal standing to trade and negotiate. She is willing to end the fighting. She refuses to trade unilateral disarmament for a verbal guarantee.

In 987 ST a shattered space straddling the border copied a human village and an Abyss hearth district at the same time. To stop either side from reading the anomaly as an invasion, she proposed a very characteristic solution — a public duel over the passage, the winner taking command of the joint party.
