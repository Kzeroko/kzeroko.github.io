The Garden is not an ordinary sky city, and it has no districts capable of supporting a society. It is an early Star-Law observation post rebuilt after the Divine Age catastrophe, holding position on the Star Tower network.

## It changes underneath you

The prologue opens only the Fallen Star Terrace, the cloud court, and the observation gallery where Selene stands. The route is white and quiet, and most players read it as a safe divine sanctuary.

With each chapter, new cracks appear, doors close, and small objects brought back from across the continent start accumulating — the vast facility gradually shows evidence that someone has been living in it.

Return visits follow a fixed rhythm: see what changed in the world, then deal with the people, then choose the next road. The Garden's functions are deliberately not collapsed into one menu — you have to walk a cloud court that looks different and hear the Star Tower load before the world's state actually feels changed.
