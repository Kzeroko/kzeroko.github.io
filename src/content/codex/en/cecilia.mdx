She is not much for fine speeches. What she does instead is check the load rating on bridges herself, learn every wounded soldier's name, and put her own signature on the loss reports. Dawn training is not only swordwork either — she runs evacuation routes with the recruits, practises carrying casualties, practises counting off in heavy smoke.

She became commander at twenty-one and still doubts she has earned it. But she is clear about one thing: that doubt is not allowed to travel downhill to her subordinates. So she puts it away and goes on getting each thing right.

If you go down on a battlefield she will shout you back onto your feet in a tone harsher than any order. Once everyone is safe, she will add, embarrassed, "…that came out too hard. Sorry."

## How she got here

Zephyris is an old Lanviris knightly house. Her father Adrian commanded the White Rose Knights before her.

In 979 ST a border withdrawal case saw Adrian accused of forging orders and losing a village. He died in prison the following year. Her brother Roderick vanished afterwards, and the family's name collapsed.

She was fourteen. Over the six years that followed she started again as a squire and won back both the house and her place in the order, one rank at a time. In 986 ST she became one of the youngest commanders in the White Rose's history.

She insists on reading every equipment maintenance record personally — because the hinge of her father's old case was a set of forged ordnance receipts.

In 987 ST she handled an attack on an escort party outside Lucentrystel, and pressed the question everyone else had skipped: why was that new rift not marked on the guild map?
