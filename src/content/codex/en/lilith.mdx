Her job is to inspect every letter, every contract, every vial and every gift the Court receives, and confirm there is no curse hidden in it. Most of the time the work is extremely dull — a whole day's output is often a single word: none.

She dislikes hearing the work called sinister. A city this short on resources cannot afford to burn everything suspicious, so someone has to look at each item, one at a time. She has done it for seven years and written something other than "none" exactly twice.

She speaks quietly, and habitually explains where the danger is before deciding whether to explain herself. Her humour is faint, and usually lands half a beat after you have stopped listening for it.

## How she got here

She was born into a small demon clan on the northern frontier. When she was eight, the clan was destroyed.

Rhavaniel carried her out of the site. The following year she was given the Asmodeus name — which caused no small argument inside the Court, and there are still people who do not recognise the adoption.

Her answer to that was simple: thirteen years of work, and an entrance examination into the Night Veil passed on her own record. Her position was not handed to her by her sister, and that matters to her more than to anyone else.

In 987 ST she laid a containment across a border rift that held back the human and demon armies at once. Anyone willing to look closely would have found that half the curses in it were channelling the rift's contamination toward uninhabited ground.

That year, the first person to see it was a stranger.
