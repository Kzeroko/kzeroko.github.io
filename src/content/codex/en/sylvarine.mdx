Since then her name has been forbidden inside the forest, and she herself went to an organisation most nations deny exists. Her work is the category ordinary courts cannot try: curses that rewrite a witness's memory, formulae that copy themselves, Star Tower incidents that leave a city holding two histories at once.

She says very little. On an operation any word can become a spell's medium, and after long enough, silence becomes habit. She has not stopped feeling things, though — she remembers the smell of leaves, which shoulder a student lifts too high when drawing a bow, the temperature someone likes their tea. She is simply not used to translating "I remember" into "I care".

Walking behind her is very safe. Her staff comes down first, and then the sound around you stops.

## How she got here

She was the finest mage of the Moonshadow Conclave, and the previous queen's closest friend.

She was also the first teacher of two children — twin sisters, one of whom became the queen and one the crown regent. She taught them for over a century, and consistently refused to teach Aethelind the high formulae, keeping her instead in places where no spirit answered, practising the bow over and over. That left a wound in Aethelind that has lasted a hundred and twenty-eight years. Sylvarine has never explained it.

In 859 ST came the White Crown Upheaval. She killed the previous queen and was declared a regicide and a deserter by the Conclave. She did not offer a single word in her defence.

Afterwards she joined the Null Covenant and became one of the very few field agents to master the Zero Law — a power that briefly severs a target's connection to the current rules of magic. No sound, no light. Only a gap where something was.
