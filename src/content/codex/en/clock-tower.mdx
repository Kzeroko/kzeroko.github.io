## What it claims

The Tower's oath is that **a conclusion may not precede its evidence, and a crown may not rewrite a formula**.

It admits anyone who can pay the fees, pass the exams, or contribute equivalent research, and does not refuse entry on nationality or faith. For many non-human scholars it is one of the few institutions on the continent where formal standing can be earned on ability.

## The edge of neutrality

Neutral is not the same as having no position. The Tower will refuse to falsify an assessment, and refuse to hand a researcher to a tribunal with no evidence.

But when research ends up used in a war, the Tower has long deflected patent and supply responsibility onto "the user is accountable" — and there is still no internal consensus about where that line actually belongs.
