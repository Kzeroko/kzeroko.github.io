## What it claims

The See teaches that suffering comes from an original sin — humanity's estrangement from the Holy Light — and that purification, service and order return a soul to it.

The Light is not fiction. Successive generations of the See really have preserved the most mature systems of healing, purification and post-disaster care in existence. Ordinary believers are not being credulous: at the worst moment of their lives, somebody genuinely did carry medicine and hot water out of a church.

## Where it goes wrong

Post-Divine-Age political narrative gradually equated demons and the dark element with that original sin, which means **the medical apparatus and the apparatus of exclusion grew up inside the same paperwork**.

The file that lets someone be treated and the file that lets someone be turned away are frequently the same file.
