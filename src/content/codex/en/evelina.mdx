Evelina can see the line in a spell formula that does not hold. What looks to everyone else like a seamless array looks to her like a page with a typo in it — and she will reach out and strike the wrong stroke through.

She runs the most consequential academic institution on the continent. Magitech standards, accident assessments and spell notation all come out of here. But the moment she meets a phenomenon she has not seen before, the Master of the Tower evaporates: she leans in, her eyes light up, she races through three hypotheses in one breath, and only then remembers to ask, “ah — did I interrupt you?”

Every apprentice in the Tower knows one thing: an unexplained small error at the end of a report gets a reply from the Master far faster than ten pages of flattery.

## How she got here

She was born in Kaleidoscope, a free city that asks nothing about your birth or your faith as long as you can pay the fees and pass the exams.

As a young woman she took one of the Twelve Seats with a paper nobody could follow, and spent the years since turning a loose old academy into the continent's standards authority. A caravan can cross three borders today on a single certificate because of the work she did then.

Her patent income is enormous. Almost all of it goes into the accident fund and observatory maintenance. She herself has close to no idea what ordinary things cost — the Tower buys her food and clothes centrally, and her companions find this endlessly funny.

In 987 ST a large mana incident struck Kaleidoscope. She ended the chaos with overwhelming counter-formulae, and picked one person out of the crowd at a glance: someone whose mana statistics did not belong to this world.
