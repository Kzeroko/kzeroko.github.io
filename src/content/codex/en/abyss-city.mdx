Abyss City sits inside an enormous volcanic rift. Ash cloud usually covers the sky, and the roads are lit by geothermal light and dark crystal.

## What you expect to see

Human rumour prepares you for a dark castle. What you actually see first is the water-ticket queue, heat-well workers, and children reading the alarm smoke.

The route takes you from the outer ash gate through the ration street and the four hearth rings up to the throne terrace, explaining step by step exactly what the [Demon Lord's](/en/codex/rhavaniel/) authority is built on.

## How the map works

Concentric rings at different elevations. Gravity lifts connect the hearth districts, and a power failure or an unstable core changes your vertical routes.

While relations are hostile you are tightly guided. The residential districts and the underground heat wells only open once trust is established.
