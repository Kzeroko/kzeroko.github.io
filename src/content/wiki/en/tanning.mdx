A water-filled cauldron is a tanning vat. You work a batch of up to 16 pieces in the world, with no screen open, through six stages: soak, flesh, tan, smoke, dry and finish. Your workmanship decides the leather's grade, from Common to Legendary, and the hide and your tools set how high it can go.

## What a batch yields

Each stage scores 0–1 and carries a share of a 100-point workmanship score; smoking adds a bonus on top. The total picks the grade.

| Grade | Workmanship | Leather |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | below 40 | Rawhide Scraps |
| Uncommon | 40 | Bundled Tanned Hide |
| Rare | 58 | Tawed Vellum Roll |
| Epic | 72 | Supple Leather |
| Noble | 84 | Tooled Noble Leather |
| Legendary | 93 | Azure Royal Leather |

The leather you receive is the lowest of three grades: the one your workmanship earned, the hide's limit and your kit's limit. A batch that scores 97 or more is a masterwork and yields one extra piece.

| Stage | Share | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soak | 12 | Lift the hide out as the water line crosses the gold band. |
| Flesh | 26 | Strike as the blade crosses each gold patch of fat. |
| Tan | 26 | Stir the liquor to keep it even, then lift the hide at its target tone. |
| Smoke | bonus, up to 8 | Hold the damper to keep the smoke in the band. Needs a fire under the vat. |
| Dry | 18 | Take up the lacing before it sags below the band. |
| Finish | 18 | Strike as each finishing sweep meets its mark, once per pass. |

## Working a batch

<Steps>

1. **Fill a cauldron with water.** A cauldron without water refuses a batch, and every finished batch uses one level of water.

2. **Carry the kit.** You need a Tanner's Round Knife and one tanning agent per 4 pieces, anywhere in your inventory or off hand.

3. **Use the vat with hides in your main hand.** The batch takes as many pieces as the held stack makes, up to 16 and up to what your tanning agents cover. Sneak while using it to finish the whole batch steadily at once.

4. **Work each stage at the vat.** The overlay at the top of the screen shows the six stage medallions, the progress belt, the working groove and your hide's colour. Each stage opens with 14 ticks (0.7 seconds) to read the instruction and closes with 12 ticks (0.6 seconds) showing your rating.

5. **Collect the leather.** It goes straight into your inventory, with the result held on the overlay for a few seconds.

</Steps>

Looking at a vat with hides or a tanning tool in hand shows the batch before you start: pieces, difficulty, target tone, water, fire, air, both grade limits and which tools count.

### Controls at the vat

Your hands only count while you look at the vat you are working.

| Input | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Attack | The stage's work action: scrape, stir, take up the lacing or strike. A click is one action; holding the key repeats nothing and never breaks the vat. |
| Use | Lifts the hide during soaking and tanning, once per stage. |
| Hold use | Holds the smoke damper down while smoking. |
| Sneak + use | Finishes every stage you have not played at the steady score. |

Finishing steadily scores 0.5 per remaining stage, so a fully steady batch is Uncommon. Stepping more than 8 blocks from the vat, breaking it or emptying it sets the batch aside. Nothing is taken until a batch finishes, so an abandoned batch costs nothing.

### The stages

**Soak.** The water line creeps and then rushes upward. Lifting inside the gold band scores 0.6 to 1, best at its centre; letting it reach the brim waterlogs the hide.

**Flesh.** The blade sweeps back and forth along the groove. A stroke inside the patch scrapes it clean and the next patch appears away from the blade; a stroke beside it nicks the hide and costs 0.04, up to 0.35. Clean strokes needed rise from 5 to 8 with the hide's difficulty.

**Tan.** The liquor settles into uneven pockets while the hide deepens towards its tone. Stir to keep evenness above 0.6; stirring a liquor that is already even splashes it. Half the score is the lift, half is how even you kept it.

**Smoke.** Only runs over a fire. The fire keeps pushing smoke up and it gusts on its own; hold the damper to thin it. Time in the band cures the hide, and smoke above 0.88 scorches it.

**Dry.** The hide slackens on its lacing. Each take-up raises tension by a fixed step; it dries only while taut and tears at the holes from 0.96.

**Finish.** One pass for buffing, plus one per finishing tool your kit counts. Each pass shows its tool, then a single sweep crosses the hide.

### How demanding a hide is

Each hide has a difficulty from 1 to 5. Faster motion and narrower windows make a high difficulty harder; the windows are set in ticks of timing.

| Difficulty | Strike window | Lift window | Clean strokes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ±2.6 ticks | ±3.2 ticks | 5 |
| 2 | ±2.2 ticks | ±2.7 ticks | 6 |
| 3 | ±1.9 ticks | ±2.3 ticks | 6 |
| 4 | ±1.65 ticks | ±2.0 ticks | 7 |
| 5 | ±1.4 ticks | ±1.7 ticks | 8 |

A stage left unattended still ends: soaking and tanning reach the brim, fleshing stops after 600 ticks (30 seconds), smoking after 400 ticks (20 seconds) and drying after 500 ticks (25 seconds).

## The tanner's kit

The round knife is required. Each finishing tool raises your kit's limit by one grade, but only on top of every tool before it in the ladder, and only those tools add a finishing pass. Every finished batch wears each tool it used by one.

| Tool | Kit limit with it | Uses | Crafted from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tanner's Round Knife | Uncommon | 128 | 2 iron ingots, stick, leather |
| Leather Shears | Rare | 128 | 2 iron ingots, Bundled Tanned Hide |
| Skiving Knife | Epic | 96 | Iron ingot, gold ingot, stick, Tawed Vellum Roll |
| Leather Awl | Noble | 80 | Diamond, stick, Supple Leather |
| Waxed Thread & Needle | Legendary | 64 | Gold ingot, 2 string, honeycomb, Tooled Noble Leather |

Each tool's recipe takes leather of the grade just below the one it opens, so the kit is built by tanning. All five recipes are shapeless crafting recipes. Creative mode wears no tools.

## Vat, fire and air

| Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| No fire under the vat | Smoking is skipped, and its medallion shows that. |
| Campfire or fire | Smoking runs with ordinary gusts. |
| Soul campfire or soul fire | Smoke gusts 30% less. |
| Lava or magma block | Smoke gusts 35% more and creeps upward; the bonus is ×1.1. |
| Spirit Brazier at its aether setting | Smoke gusts 40% less; the bonus is ×1.25. |
| Open sky in daylight, or an ultrawarm dimension such as the Nether | Drying runs ×1.4 faster and the lacing slackens more slowly. |
| Rain falling on the vat | Drying runs ×0.7 and the lacing slackens faster. |

The fire is read with the same rules as the Aether Crucible, so a Spirit Brazier under the vat counts as the fire it is set to. Fire is read when smoking begins and air when drying begins; a fire put out before then skips smoking.

Tanning agents are bark and wood: Farmer's Delight tree bark when that mod is present, and oak, spruce, dark oak and mangrove logs. Bark is used before logs. Finished batches count as recipe completions with source `isekaiexpansion:tanning` for [quest objectives](/en/wiki/quest-objectives/).
