KTM2 runs four independent level tracks. They are not variations of the same number: each one gates a different kind of content, and progress on one does not carry over to another.

| Track | Range | What it gates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player | 1–100 | Skill tree nodes, identity progression, most gear requirements |
| Equipment | 1–100 | Socket slots locked below their minimum item level |
| Adventurer rank | 0–8 | Commissions, districts, some skill tree branches |
| NPC affinity | 0–11 | What a companion is willing to tell you |

## Player level

The curve is deliberately in three phases: a fast opening so early sessions always produce a level, a long linear plateau through the middle game, and a steep final stretch where 91→100 alone costs more than 1→50.

## Equipment level

Every piece of gear levels on its own. The important consequence is that **socket slots unlock with item level**: a socket whose profile sets a minimum item level stays locked until the item reaches it, no matter what gem you are holding.

<LevelTable track="equipment" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Use one weapon, not five">
The equipment curve is steeper than the player curve at the top end. Spreading experience across an armoury means nothing reaches its late sockets.
</Callout>

## Adventurer rank

Rank starts at **0** and becomes rank 1 the moment the Adventurer's Guild registers you — the first 1,200 points are effectively your paperwork. From there each rank costs roughly 1.55× the last, so rank 8 is a long-term goal rather than a mid-game milestone.

## NPC affinity

Affinity is tracked per character and moves through four named stages. They describe **how much someone lets you know and share**, not romance, and not damage.

<LevelTable track="affinity" />

<Callout type="note" title="What affinity does and does not do">
Deeper stages change what a companion tells you, what she will let you help with, and which of her problems she stops hiding. They do not turn her into a better party member statistically, and they do not resolve her personal story on their own.
</Callout>
