New worlds prepare one Remote Sky Garden in the Overworld before players can enter. You first arrive on its lower terrace, facing the sanctuary. The Teleport Stone starts with this hub as its destination.

## Arrival at the garden

The authored marker places your feet on the terrace's horizontal plane. Its default radius is `10` blocks; each arrival chooses a safe cell within that circle and favours unoccupied cells. It checks dry support, body clearance, hazards and the world border. It never searches the roofs or terrain below the island. If no safe cell remains, arrival fails instead of dropping you elsewhere.

The marker becomes its authored gold floor after preparation. Normal reconnects retain your saved position. A valid personal respawn point keeps its usual precedence; returning to the world spawn uses the terrace again. The hub remains available in the [Teleport Stone ledger](/en/wiki/teleport-stones/) without occupying any of its `16` anchor slots.

## Location and height

The original semi-random land survey chooses a regional starting point. The garden then searches around it for an offshore site. Generated blocks beneath the complete footprint are inspected before placement. The arrival plane starts at Y `576` or higher; the lowest island block keeps at least `64` blocks of clearance above existing terrain and objects. A site is rejected when the full structure cannot fit below the build ceiling.

Offshore placement keeps the direct overhead shadow on water. The surrounding ocean buffer is sampled, not cleared or replaced. Sun-angle shadows and shader behaviour remain controlled by the installed renderer; this feature does not change sunlight, caves or shader code.

The minimum Overworld build ceiling defaults to `1024`, exclusive, and never cuts down the terrain preset's existing height. Extra build space does not stretch terrain or move sea level. Configure height before creating a world; see [World spawn configuration](/en/wiki/world-spawn-configuration/).

## Saving and loading

The entire garden is prepared during world loading. Its reservation and resource fingerprint are saved, then all placed chunks are flushed before the hub is marked ready. Interrupted preparation repeats at the same reservation before players can join. A completed garden is not pasted again on reconnect or restart, preserving later edits.

The hub's three-dimensional bounds share the normal protected-zone rules and exclude unmanaged natural mob spawns. Terrain and water below the island are outside that zone. Commands, spawn eggs and authored encounters retain their existing rules.

## Ground spawn mode

Disable `spawn.skyGarden.enabled` before creating a world to retain the existing semi-random ground spawn. Its biome, elevation, shoreline, settlement and hostile-structure checks remain configurable. Unsupported generators or an unsuccessful ground search leave vanilla selection in charge. With the hub enabled, failure to prepare a valid site stops loading with a diagnostic.

Administrators with permission level `2` can run `/ise spawn survey` to inspect ground candidates or `/ise spawn relocate` to move the world spawn to a newly selected ground point. These commands do not move or duplicate the garden. Both use the Overworld even when run from another dimension. Full settings are in [World spawn configuration](/en/wiki/world-spawn-configuration/); marker authoring is in [Remote Sky Garden runtime](/en/dev/remote-sky-garden/).
