Talents give each character an innate rank from E to S. Practice can add up to 2 ranks, subject to that talent's hidden potential and the S ceiling; the effective rank controls its current bonuses even before the talent is revealed on screen. Spell schools can go further: once a school stands at S, practice keeps adding levels of S+ without end. Astral Energy Capacity grows differently again, widening the reserve a little with every level.

## What each rank changes

### Weapons and dual wielding

| Rank | Melee damage | Ranged damage | Melee speed per held weapon | Dual-wield damage | Dual-wield speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E | ×0.80 | ×0.86 | −0.15 | ×0.84 | −0.20 |
| D | ×0.90 | ×0.93 | −0.08 | ×0.92 | −0.10 |
| C | ×1.00 | ×1.00 | 0 | ×1.00 | 0 |
| B | ×1.06 | ×1.05 | 0 | ×1.07 | +0.07 |
| A | ×1.12 | ×1.10 | +0.05 | ×1.14 | +0.14 |
| S | ×1.20 | ×1.16 | +0.10 | ×1.22 | +0.22 |

Damage entries are multipliers. Speed entries add directly to the attack-speed attribute; they are not percentage multipliers, even though the current talent screen formats them as percentages. Each hand's recognised melee weapon contributes its own speed entry. While both hands hold recognised, non-two-handed Better Combat weapons, dual-wield damage multiplies the weapon's melee bonus and dual-wield speed adds once.

For example, S weapon talent with S dual-wield talent gives ×1.20 ×1.22 = ×1.464 talent damage before other combat modifiers. Two S melee weapon talents plus S dual wield add +0.10 +0.10 +0.22 = +0.42 attack speed.

### Spell schools

| Rank | Spell damage | Cast time | Energy cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E | ×0.80 | ×1.50 | ×1.40 |
| D | ×0.90 | ×1.25 | ×1.10 |
| C | ×1.00 | ×1.00 | ×1.00 |
| B | ×1.06 | ×1.00 | ×0.85 |
| A | ×1.12 | ×1.00 | ×0.75 |
| S | ×1.20 | ×0.75 | ×0.50 |
| S+ | ×1.25, rising | ×0.70, falling | ×0.45, falling |

Spell-school rank affects supported spell damage, positive cast duration for non-channelled spells, and energy cost. Healing is not multiplied merely because the spell-damage column exists.

S+ improves with its level. Each level of S+ adds power: 1 point per level for the first 10 levels, then 10 × ln(1 + (level − 10) ÷ 10) more, so the gains never stop but grow smaller. Spell damage is ×(1.25 + 0.006 × power), cast time ×0.70 ÷ (1 + 0.015 × power) and energy cost ×0.45 ÷ (1 + 0.02 × power). At S+ 10 that is ×1.31 damage, ×0.61 cast time and ×0.38 energy.

### Astral Energy Capacity

| Rolled rank | Base reserve | Reserve per level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E | ×0.60 | +1 |
| D | ×0.80 | +1.25 |
| C | ×1.00 | +1.5 |
| B | ×1.30 | +2 |
| A | ×1.60 | +2.5 |
| S | ×2.00 | +3 |

Maximum Astral Energy is the attribute's base value × the rolled rank's base reserve, plus the reserve per level × the capacity level. The base value is 30, so a C channel at level 40 holds 30 + 60 = 90. The rank shown for this talent stays the rolled one; its level has no ceiling. Other capacity modifiers remain separate.

Reconnecting preserves saved Astral Energy. Respawning restores the capacity talent; if lost equipment leaves a genuinely lower final capacity, excess energy is clamped to that capacity.

Spell tiers run from 1 to 13 and ask for one rank per pair: E for tiers 1–2, D for 3–4, C for 5–6, B for 7–8, A for 9–10, S for 11–12, and S+ for tier 13. These checks apply to the mapped schools when binding or casting; creative players bypass the talent requirement. A school without a talent mapping passes that particular check. A woven [spell formula](/en/wiki/spell-formulas/) checks each spell's tuned tier and the formula's own tier instead.

## Practice and your ceiling

| Milestone | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Proficiency 0–3 | No earned rank yet. |
| Proficiency 4 | First earned rank, if potential permits. |
| Proficiency 9 | Second earned rank, if potential permits. |
| Innate S, or no growth potential | No trainable ladder for a weapon talent. |
| Room for only 1 rank | The ladder stops at proficiency 4. |
| Room for 2 ranks | The ladder stops at proficiency 9. |
| A spell school standing at S | Every further level is a level of S+, without end. |

A talent can have room for 0, 1 or 2 earned ranks. S is the upper limit of that ladder: an innate A talent can gain only 1 rank even with deep potential. Practice already earned changes the effective rank; it does not replace the innate rank.

A spell school whose ladder reaches S keeps training. Its next level reads S+, and the levels after it count on as S+ 2, S+ 3 and so on: the first level of S+ costs 6,000 practice and each later one 1,500 more than the one before. An innate S school has no ladder below S, so its practice goes straight toward S+; a school whose potential stops short of S never reaches it.

Astral Energy Capacity has no ladder of ranks. Its first level costs 60 practice and each later one 15 more.

The cost of a proficiency step increases with proficiency and effective rank. Potential changes the extra cost above E; it does not reduce incoming practice awards. The first step at effective E costs 90 practice. The displayed next-step cost is the cost for that character, not a universal cost for everyone using the same weapon. At the talent's ceiling, further awards stop and leftover practice is cleared by normal levelling.

| Practice source | Award | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognised melee damage processing | 8 | Trains the associated melee weapon talent. |
| The same melee processing while dual wielding | 8 | Also trains Twin-Blade Coordination; it does not require a hit from each hand first. |
| Recognised ranged damage processing | 11 | Trains the associated ranged weapon talent. |
| Magic spell release with progress greater than 0.30 | 10 | Trains the mapped spell school. Weapon arts do not award. |
| Any payment of Astral Energy for a spell | 2 + 60 × L × (1 + 2L) × (1 + 3D³) | Trains Astral Energy Capacity. L is the cost as a share of the reserve, at most 1; D is how empty the reserve is left, from 0 when full to 1 when dry. |

Awards require a server-side player outside creative/spectator mode, an existing talent entry and remaining room to grow. Visibility is not a requirement: unrevealed talents can still receive practice. Weapon awards happen during qualifying damage processing, not once per swing or only after a final positive health loss; they have no separate per-target cooldown here. Channel pulses alone do not give the school award, but every pulse that pays energy trains the reserve.

The reserve rewards spending it hard. A spell that costs 5 of a full 100 earns 5 practice; one that costs 40 earns 53; the same 40 paid from 45 earns 156.

## Discovering and reading talents

Open the talent tab from the inventory's extended tabs. Left-click a card to select its details; right-click to open its dossier. Escape closes the dossier first. The screen does not pause the game and captures its values when opened, so reopen it to inspect newly synchronised progress.

Melee talents reveal after a qualifying attack action has at least one target. The dual-wield talent reveals through that same action when both weapons qualify. Ranged talents reveal on a qualifying shot at full draw progress of at least 1. Revealing a talent changes its visibility, not its rank.

The Astral Energy and spell-school entries belong to the crystal-hidden group. The first qualifying release of a school's spell, or the first paid pulse of its channel, reveals that school's talent and Astral Energy Capacity before the energy is paid, with the same notice a weapon talent shows. The operator command `/ise talent reveal crystal <target>` reveals the whole group at once; no identification-crystal item interaction is wired to it.

The card's practice band measures progress toward that talent's own reachable ceiling. The dossier separates proficiency level from practice toward the next step and describes whether potential is fixed, shallow, deep or spent. Raw potential is not displayed. An innate S weapon talent has no ladder; a talent that reached its growth ceiling shows completion rather than promising more trainable levels. A school on S+ shows its S+ level and the practice toward the next one, and Astral Energy Capacity shows its level and the reserve its levels have added. Hidden cards conceal their bonuses and mastery details.

## Interactions and data

| System | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Talents are separate from race, class and profession selection. |
| Combat | Weapon type and spell-school attribution determine which rank applies. |
| Skill requirements | A talent-rank requirement reads the effective rank after practice. |
| Respawn | Talent progress, reveal state and saved Astral Energy copy to the new player; the capacity talent is reapplied. |
| Operator reroll | Replaces ranks, potential and practice; reveal state is separate. |

Definitions and training rules are registered by the mod rather than loaded from a talent JSON directory.
