Attributes describe your current capabilities; status effects temporarily change them or enable special behaviour. This guide separates damage, recovery, control and social stats, with the complete [status-effect catalogue](/en/wiki/status-effects/) on its own page.

## Choosing useful stats

| Goal | Relevant stats |
| --- | --- |
| Sustain attacks and spells | Astral Energy capacity, recovery and Energy Leech. |
| Deal more damage at high or low health | Stamina for current health; Enmity for missing health. |
| Break through defence | Armour Penetration for physical hits; Magic Penetration for generic spell resistance. |
| Keep a shield usable | Shield Power, maximum shield gauge and shield recovery. |
| Resist control | Tenacity for duration and harmful-effect resistance. |
| Improve authored social checks | Eloquence and Charm; check each dialogue's actual requirement. |

## Tamed companions

Your status screen shows occupied companion slots and your current limit in the progression metrics. Taming a wolf, cat, parrot or horse-family animal reserves a slot for that entity. Modded animals inheriting those taming behaviours use the same limit. Morph forms do not reserve slots.

Companions keep their slots in unloaded chunks and other dimensions, and across server restarts. A full roster prevents another tame. Reducing the attribute below the occupied count keeps your existing companions; you must free enough slots before taming another.

Death frees the slot and sends you the companion's name. If you are offline, the server keeps the notice, refreshes your occupied count when you join and then clears the queued notice. Releasing ownership also frees a slot. Chunk unloading and dimension travel do not release ownership.

## Energy and shield recovery

Astral Energy regenerates 1 point per pulse. The pulse interval is the integer part of `20 / energy_regen_speed` ticks, with at most 1 pulse per tick; energy removal pauses recovery for 20 ticks (1 second). Maximum energy is read as an integer. Energy Inefficiency makes the normal energy check fail even if the reserve is full.

Shield Power multiplies base shield capacity and the recovery amount, and divides guard cost. At its neutral value, recovery is 5 gauge per pulse; the interval is `max(1, round(20 / shield_gauge_regen_speed))` ticks. Blocking locks recovery for 40 ticks (2 seconds). Empty gauge or a blow above the equipped shield's crushing threshold breaks guard and starts that shield's cooldown. A cooling shield does not regenerate.

Shield recovery can temporarily overshoot capacity by a pulse, then be clamped on the next tick. Different shields supply different block thresholds and costs; the gauge attribute alone does not describe a shield's complete protection.

## Percentages and damage

Many direct combat stats accept a ratio or percentage-point representation internally: an absolute value greater than 1 is divided by 100; values from -1 through 1 are used directly. Thus `0.25` and `25` both mean 25%, but exactly `1` means 100%, not 1%. This convention does not apply to every attribute: critical-stat forwarding uses the explicit factors above, and Ability Haste uses its own formula.

With current-health fraction `h`, the Enmity/Stamina multiplier is `1 + Enmity × (1 - h) + Stamina × h`, after percentage normalisation. Shield-break and Ultimate Burst bonuses multiply separately when their conditions hold.

Omnivamp uses the positive amount passed to the post-damage hook, rather than measuring the victim's health before and after. Only the first qualifying hit in a tick has full recovery; later hits from that attacker use 22%. Healing Amplification can then increase that healing.

Environmental Resistance covers in-fire, on-fire, lava, hot floor, freezing, drying out, cactus and sweet berry bush damage. Drowning and starvation use a separate damage rule based on 10% of maximum health. Element weaknesses and resistances are another independent stage; see [elements](/en/wiki/elements/).

## Control, stealth and survival

Harmful effects from another living source roll against `clamp(1 - Tenacity + Debuff Accuracy, 0.05, 1)`. At 100% accuracy, the random check is bypassed; team protection and other application rules still apply. Effects without such a source do not make this accuracy roll.

Tenacity shortens effects classified as crowd control, plus Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue, Blindness, Levitation and Nausea. The shortened duration is rounded and has a minimum of 1 tick. It does not shorten every harmful effect.

Passive hostile detection combines the strongest active stealth restriction with Stealth Efficiency. Partial stealth keeps a minimum 2-block detection range; complete detection prevention can reject it entirely. Shadow Phase and Phantom Veil also break when an eligible hostile enters their configured nearby search box, so reduced visibility is not a universal immunity.

Guts requires health above 25% before the fatal hit. Death Defiance bypasses that health threshold and forces the chance to 100%; neither protects against the void, sickness or potion sickness. A successful intervention sets health to 1.
