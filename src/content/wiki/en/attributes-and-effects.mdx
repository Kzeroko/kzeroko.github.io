IsekaiExpansion registers **44 `isekaiexpansion:*` attributes** and **7 custom `spell_power:*` school attributes**. The ranges and defaults below are the shipped values.

## Resource & shield

| Attribute | Range / default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_energy` | `0–10000`, default `30` | Astral Energy cap. Current energy is clamped down if it exceeds the cap. |
| `energy_regen_speed` | `0.2–200`, default `0.5` | Passive refill interval, `regen_ticks = 20 / value`. Higher is faster. |
| `max_shield_gauge` | `0–1000`, default `75` | Base shield gauge before `shield_power` is applied. |
| `shield_gauge_regen_speed` | `0.5–10`, default `2` | Gauge refill interval, `max(1, round(20 / value))` ticks. |

## Combat, recovery, threat & control

| Attribute | Range / default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ability_haste` | `-99–500`, default `0` | Reduces skill cooldowns with haste math. Negative haste **lengthens** them. |
| `armor_penetration` | `0–100`, default `0` | Physical hits ignore part of target armour before vanilla reduction. Applies to melee, ranged and the non-magic fallback category. |
| `magic_penetration` | `0–100`, default `0` | Spell damage ignores part of generic Spell Power resistance. Does **not** reduce school-specific resistances. |
| `tenacity` | `0–100`, default `0` | Shortens crowd control and makes hostile effects harder to land. Reduction caps at 95%. |
| `enmity` | `0–100`, default `0` | Increases outgoing damage **at low health**. |
| `stamina` | `0–100`, default `0` | Increases outgoing damage **at high health**. |
| `omnivamp` | `0–100`, default `0` | Heals from post-mitigation damage. Extra hits in the same tick use AOE falloff. |
| `shield_power` | `0–100`, default `0` | Raises gauge cap, gauge regen amount and guard cost efficiency. |
| `aggro_generation` | `-100–500`, default `0` | How strongly mobs retarget onto you, on hit and during Provoke. |
| `burst_damage` | `0–1000`, default `0` | Only affects damage sourced from `isekaiexpansion:ultimate_burst`. |
| `energy_leech` | `0–100`, default `0` | Restores Astral Energy after spell, melee or ranged hits. |
| `shield_break` | `0–100`, default `0` | Extra damage against a player actively guarding with usable shield gauge. |
| `evasion_counter` | `0–1000`, default `0` | When Dodge or Foresight prevents damage, lets the defender counterattack a nearby attacker. |
| `debuff_accuracy` | `0–1000`, default `0` | Pushes harmful effects through Tenacity. At 100% or more the accuracy roll is forced to pass. |
| `guts_chance` | `0–100`, default `0` | Chance to survive lethal damage at 1 HP, **only if health was above 25% before the hit**. |
| `stealth_efficiency` | `0–100`, default `0` | Shrinks hostile detection range. At 100% passive acquisition fails outright. |
| `environmental_resistance` | `0–100`, default `0` | Reduces fire/lava, hot floor, freezing, dry-out, cactus and berry bush damage — and Inversed Breathing land damage. Does **not** reduce drowning or starvation. |

<Callout type="tip" title="Enmity and Stamina are opposites on purpose">
They reward two different playstyles and stack badly together. Pick the one your build actually produces: a glass cannon that never gets hit wants Stamina; a bruiser that fights at low health wants Enmity.
</Callout>

## Critical & spell wrappers

These exist so KTM2 gear can carry one consistent stat name while the value is routed into the mod that owns the mechanic.

| Attribute | Routed into |
| --- | --- |
| `melee_crit_chance` | `playerex:melee_crit_chance` |
| `melee_crit_damage` | `playerex:melee_crit_damage` |
| `range_crit_chance` | `playerex:ranged_crit_chance` |
| `range_crit_damage` | `playerex:ranged_crit_damage` |
| `magic_crit_chance` | `spell_power:critical_chance` |
| `magic_crit_damage` | `spell_power:critical_damage` |
| `elemental_power` | fire, frost, lightning, water, wind and earth spell power |
| `dys_aether` | `elemental_power`, plus arcane, soul and dark |
| `aether_power` | `dys_aether`, plus healing, light and astral |

The last three nest: `aether_power` is the widest, feeding `dys_aether`, which feeds `elemental_power`. One point of Aether Power therefore touches every school.

## PlayerEx base stat wrappers

Seven pass-through wrappers, each `target += value × 1.0`:

`constitution` · `strength` · `dexterity` · `intelligence` · `technique` · `perception` · `belief`

### Elemental vulnerability

These mark a target as open to a specific school. Four of them are consumed by a direct hit; two persist.

| Effect | Strongly weak to | Mildly weak to | Removed by direct hit | Side effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `electrified` | Frost | Water | yes | — |
| `iced` | Water | Lightning | yes | Extinguishes the target every tick |
| `ignite` | Wind | Lightning | yes | Keeps the target on fire for 5s |
| `wet` | Earth | Frost | yes | Extinguishes the target every tick |
| `luminous` | Dark | Arcane | no | — |
| `shadowed` | Light | Arcane | no | — |

<Callout type="tip" title="This is the combo system">
Apply a vulnerability, then hit it with the matching school. The four consumable ones are meant to be spent immediately; Luminous and Shadowed persist, so they suit sustained pressure instead.
</Callout>
