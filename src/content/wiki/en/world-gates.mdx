World Gates stand among ruined arches, monoliths and broken sanctuaries in the Overworld. Each gate connects to a single distant partner. Finding an entrance opens a route to another region, while the journey to each ruin remains part of exploration.

## A route worth discovering

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ruin designs | 6 equally weighted variants |
| Portal scale | Approximately 10 blocks wide and tall |
| Natural ruin spacing | Nominally 2304 blocks; at least 2000 blocks between generated gate anchors |
| Natural partner distance | 1024–8192 blocks horizontally |
| Entry charge | 40 ticks (2 seconds) of continuous contact |

The ruins use weathered masonry, carved stone and scattered rubble. Their gates have different proportions and colours, hold a fixed orientation and emit a quiet portal ambience. They cannot be crafted, picked up or moved with pistons.

Unsuitable terrain, oceans and nearby structures can leave a region without a gate, creating larger gaps. The spacing applies to naturally generated ruins; developer-placed gates are configured separately.

## Entering a gate

Walk into the glowing surface and remain there while it prepares the destination. The first journey establishes the pair; later journeys keep the same destination. There is no destination menu. A first visit may take longer while the distant terrain loads, and leaving the surface cancels preparation.

- **While mounted:** dismount before entering.
- **After arrival:** leave the gate before trying to return; the portal cooldown prevents immediate bouncing.
- **When the destination is blocked:** travel fails until there is a clear, dry landing on a supported surface.
- **When no partner is available:** the gate reports that it cannot form a route; it does not create a replacement platform or portal.

## Exploration and other travel

| Interaction | Result |

| Global portal magic | Contact attunes you to that World Gate. |

## Where the data lives

The six templates ship under `data/isekaiexpansion/structures/world_portal_ruins/`. World generation uses the shared [structure placement format](/en/wiki/structure-placement/). Mapmakers can configure anchors inside their own landmarks using the [World Gate developer guide](/en/dev/world-gates/).
