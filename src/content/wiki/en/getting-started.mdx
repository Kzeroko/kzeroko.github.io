IsekaiExpansion supplies custom RPG systems for the KenToMahou2 (KTM2) modpack. Start with your controls, character choices and current resources, then use the linked guides when you encounter their equipment or content.

## Set up your controls

These are this mod's default bindings. Your control settings or the pack's saved options may assign different keys.

| Default | Action |
| --- | --- |
| K | Open the triggerable-skill screen. |
| Left Alt, tap | Invoke the skill currently readied on the skill rail. |
| Left Alt, hold and scroll | Browse the skill rail; releasing keeps the choice without casting it. |
| Left Alt, double tap | Open the follower command wheel when it can open. |
| J | Open the quest screen. |
| U | Open the emoji screen. |
| Unbound | Equipment-skill activation; assign a key before using its manual activation path. |

The skill key waits briefly to distinguish a tap from a double tap. Selecting a skill does not bypass its unlock, cooldown or resource requirements. Some screens and action restrictions prevent activation; see [the skill tree](/en/wiki/skill-tree/) and [equipment skills](/en/wiki/equipment-skills/).

## Understand your character choices

[Fate Balance](/en/wiki/fate-balance/) combines blessings and burdens. The default starting budget is 10 points, controlled by the server; review a choice's effects and conflicts before confirming.

[Race, class and profession](/en/wiki/identity/) are separate identity layers. Their available choices and promotion requirements come from the loaded content. Read the requirement shown for the choice you want; player level alone does not satisfy every promotion.

The [Wheel of Fate](/en/wiki/skill-tree/) is another progression surface. Player-level increases grant its main-tree points, while individual nodes can have their own requirements.

## Keep the progression tracks separate

| Track | What to look up |
| --- | --- |
| Player level | Experience and skill-tree points in [levels and experience](/en/wiki/leveling/). |
| Equipment level | Smithing-stone growth; ordinary weapon use is not the documented upgrade route. |
| Adventurer rank | Reputation requirements and explicit promotion, separate from player experience. |
| NPC favour | Relationship gates tied to that player and NPC identity. |

Equipment also has separate systems for [skill unlocks and awakening](/en/wiki/equipment-skills/), [gem sockets](/en/wiki/gems-and-sockets/) and [blueprint crafting](/en/wiki/forgery/). A socket, item level and awakened skill are not interchangeable upgrades. Read the relevant guide when you have the material or item it uses.

## Watch the resources behind combat

Astral Energy is a current reserve with a maximum and recovery rate. Shields use a separate gauge: blocking consumes it, pauses recovery and can break guard. [Attributes and effects](/en/wiki/attributes-and-effects/) explains the numbers and the [status-effect catalogue](/en/wiki/status-effects/) explains restrictions, recovery buffs and removal rules.

Some mobs carry elemental data. Match your incoming damage type against the **target's** [elements](/en/wiki/elements/); a mob without assigned elements gets no elemental multiplier. Multiple weaknesses and resistances combine, so the rule is more than a universal ±25% adjustment.

## Make a place to recover

[Comfort](/en/wiki/comfort/) tracks food variety, damage, sleep and environmental conditions. Shelter near home can offset natural decay under the conditions listed in the guide; a roof anywhere does not guarantee that result. The Comfort guide lists the conditions and the consequences of each band.

[My Home](/en/wiki/my-home/) covers claiming a recognised space, membership, furniture and qualifying sleep benefits. [Wayfinding](/en/wiki/wayfinding/) covers landmarks and navigation, while [terrain](/en/wiki/terrain/) describes the generated world and its settings.
