The Forgery Anvil holds a blueprint and 9 material slots. It supports both step-by-step recipes and smithing-stone upgrades; the finished item stays on the anvil until you collect it.

## What a strike does

| Operation | Progress |
| --- | --- |
| Recipe, bundled `isekaiexpansion:forgery_hammer` | 20 strength per accepted strike. |
| Recipe, Hundredfold Hammering active | Hammer strength × 1.5, rounded down: 30 for the bundled hammer. |
| Smithing-stone upgrade | 1 strike of progress, regardless of hammer strength. |
| Upgrade completion | 10 strikes; 5 with Hundredfold Hammering active on the recorded smith. |

Recipe steps set their own strength requirement. Hammer strength is an item property; rarity separately affects smithing-stone outcome chances.

## Bind, supply and forge

1. Reveal a [blueprint](/en/wiki/blueprints/), then use it on an unlocked anvil. The anvil stores the paper and consumes 1 held blueprint outside creative mode.
2. Insert exactly the current step's materials, in the listed order. Equal items can merge into an existing stack, so counts matter as much as item types.
3. Attack with a forging hammer while aiming at the anvil's top face. The attack checks a 1.5-block raycast and a hit at least 0.9 blocks above the anvil base.
4. When the required progress is reached, the step's inventory is consumed and progress resets. Supply the next step.
5. Use the completed anvil with an empty main hand to collect the result. The anvil keeps its blueprint for another batch.

### Interact controls

These controls read the main hand.

| Holding or condition | Top face | Other face |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | Add 1 to the first available or compatible slot. | Add as much as fits in that slot; overflow stays in hand. |
| Empty hand or forging hammer | Remove 1 from the last occupied slot. | Remove that whole stack. |
| Valid blueprint on an unlocked anvil | Bind the blueprint. | Same. |
| Completed result and empty hand | Collect the result. | Same. |
| Sneaking, empty hand, no materials, no step or progress | Retrieve the stored blueprint. | Same. |
| Sneaking, empty hand, no materials, after a step has advanced | Open the stored plan's read-only review while unfinished. | Same. |
| Dissolve Potion·Alpha | Reset the batch while keeping materials and the bound blueprint. | Same. |

A completed result takes priority over blueprint retrieval. Timed inventory items are not accepted as ordinary materials. Outside creative mode, resetting consumes 1 potion and returns its bottle; creative insertion and blueprint binding preserve the held item.

## Smithing-stone upgrades

Place exactly 1 eligible piece of equipment and at least 1 smithing stone on the anvil. No blueprint is required. This combination takes priority over recipe forging, including when a blueprint is already stored.

| Stone | ID | Base experience per item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smithing Stone Shard | `isekaiexpansion:smithing_stone_shard` | 150 |
| Smithing Stone | `isekaiexpansion:smithing_stone` | 750 |
| Smithing Stone Chunk | `isekaiexpansion:smithing_stone_chunk` | 3000 |

Armour, shields, trinkets, bows, crossbows, tridents, tools and guns qualify. Items with sockets or attribute modifiers can qualify too; smithing stones and fusible gems do not. Multiple equipment stacks or unrelated materials invalidate the combination.

| Outcome | Equipment | Stones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Success | Gains the batch's experience; Hundredfold Hammering multiplies it by 1.15, rounded to an integer. | Consumed. |
| Great success | Gains the same experience and permanent great-success stacks used by equipment stat bonuses. | Consumed. |
| Failure | Gains no experience; its failure count increases by 1. | Consumed. |
| Failure count exceeds 10 | The equipment breaks. This is the 11th accumulated failure. | Consumed. |

The warning begins at 9 failures. Success does not clear previous failures. More committed stone material, hammer rarity, luck and Hundredfold Hammering affect outcome chances; no combination guarantees success. Great success yields 1–256 stacks per batch, with a stored cap of 1000000.

Removing the upgrade combination clears its strike progress on the server tick. Changing smiths does not transfer recorded ownership to the latest striker, so finish the batch with the intended smith available.

## Resetting and recovering items

Dissolve Potion·Alpha (`isekaiexpansion:typea_potion_dissolve`) resets step, progress and smith attribution while retaining the blueprint and current materials. Previously completed steps have already consumed their materials. Remove the remaining stacks, then sneak-use the empty reset anvil to recover the blueprint.

Breaking the anvil drops its current inventory and stored blueprint. Drinking the potion instead applies damage equal to 10 times the drinker's maximum health; use it on the anvil to reset crafting.

## Where the data lives

Recipes are standard data-pack recipes with type `isekaiexpansion:forgery`; [forgery recipe data](/en/wiki/forgery-recipes/) documents every field. [Blueprint data](/en/wiki/blueprint-data/) connects a researched plan to a recipe, and [levels and experience](/en/wiki/leveling/) explains equipment progression. Developers can inspect [forgery runtime](/en/dev/forgery-runtime/) for persistence, upgrade formulas and event contracts.
