A blueprint stores its own discovery and research. Give the paper to another player, put it in a chest or save and reload: its recorded progress travels with the item.

## What research unlocks

| State | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Empty | Discover a supported reference item held in the opposite hand. |
| Unidentified | Transcribe evidence into its required knowledge domains. |
| Revealed | View the recipe, bind it to a forgery anvil or request a blacksmith commission. |

A revealed plan does not grant a permanent player unlock. Keep the blueprint for [forgery](/en/wiki/forgery/) or [commissioning](/en/wiki/blacksmith-commissions/).

## Discover and transcribe

1. Hold an empty `isekaiexpansion:blueprint` in one hand and a supported reference item in the other. Use the blueprint.
2. Discovery consumes the definition's reference count and binds the sheet to that blueprint.
3. Open the unidentified plan and choose an unfinished knowledge domain. Its evidence comes from the main inventory, including the hotbar.
4. Transcribe. The server checks the currently held blueprint and evidence again, consumes the full source count and adds insight up to the domain's requirement.
5. Complete every domain to reveal the plan. Use it normally to view the recipe, or sneak while using it to return to the research view.

Evidence sources have an authored priority: the first source with a sufficient stack wins, then the first matching main-inventory slot. The system does not combine partial stacks or search the offhand for evidence. It does not choose the most efficient source automatically.

**Creative mode** still requires matching reference items and evidence counts, but does not consume them. **A completed domain** consumes no further evidence. **A final transcription** still consumes the full source count even when only part of its insight is needed.

### Bundled diamond-sword example

| Requirement | Insight | Example evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `isekaiexpansion:metallurgy` | 24 | Iron ingot: 4 per item. |
| `isekaiexpansion:weaponcraft` | 12 | Flint: 2 per item; iron sword: 12 per item. |

Discover this plan with a diamond sword. Evidence alternatives follow their configured priority, so an iron sword is not selected ahead of available flint. These are bundled values; a server data pack can change them.

## Using the plan at an anvil

An unrevealed plan cannot supply a recipe. A revealed plan must point to an available forgery recipe before an empty, unlocked anvil will accept it. Binding stores a copy on the anvil and consumes the held blueprint outside creative mode.

Sneak-use the anvil with an empty hand to retrieve a bound blueprint before any step or progress has started, provided no ingredients remain. After a step has advanced, that same interaction opens a read-only recipe review when the anvil contains no ingredients and forging is unfinished. It does not allow transcription against the stored item. Completed output takes priority over these interactions.

## Changes to research data

Opening a bound plan refreshes its metadata and checks its saved progress against current requirements. A data pack can add requirements and make a previously revealed plan incomplete again. If its definition disappears, the bound sheet cannot be rediscovered as another plan automatically.

Legacy items containing only `BlueprintRecipeId` are not migrated into revealed research plans by the current implementation.

## Where the data lives

Knowledge sources live in `data/<namespace>/knowledge/`; plans live in `data/<namespace>/blueprints/`. [Blueprint and knowledge data](/en/wiki/blueprint-data/) covers every field, validation and reload behaviour.
