Comfort is a character value from 0 to 100. Meals, damage, sleep and the environment change it; its current band affects movement, block breaking and an extra periodic hunger cost.

## The three bands

| Band | Range | Movement | Block breaking | Extra hunger exhaustion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Painful** | 0–40 | −20% base | ×0.90 | ×1.50 |
| **Normal** | 41–70 | unchanged | ×1.00 | ×1.00 |
| **Relaxed** | 71–100 | +20% base | ×1.10 | ×0.50 |

The exhaustion column applies to the extra Comfort cost, not every source of hunger exhaustion. Outside Creative and Spectator modes, that cost is evaluated every 60 ticks (3 seconds): base 0.30 while sprinting, 0.10 while stationary on the ground or in a vehicle, and 0.15 otherwise. The current band's multiplier then applies.

## Natural change

Above 0 Comfort, natural change normally runs every 1200 ticks (60 seconds). Each evaluation applies −1 plus an environment correction clamped to −5…+1, before fate modifiers. A +1 correction cancels the base loss; the lowest correction produces −6 before those modifiers.

### Environment correction

| Condition | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Sky hidden and within 16 blocks of your same-dimension respawn point or the current world's spawn | +1 |
| Rain, visible sky and no helmet | −1 |
| Snow-precipitation biome at your position without full armour | −1 |
| Nether with full armour | −1 |
| Nether without full armour | −3 |

The snow check does not require active snowfall. Full armour means every armour slot is occupied.

### When natural decay pauses

The natural-change timer pauses in Creative or Spectator mode, while sleeping, or while Farmer's Delight `comfort` is active. This does not block food, damage or other direct Comfort changes. At 0 Comfort the timer also stops.

### Nourishment

Farmer's Delight `nourishment` multiplies the interval by ×1.5: the ordinary 60 seconds becomes 90 seconds. This combines with the fate interval multiplier below. The integration checks the effect's presence; the effect must be registered and active.

## Food

<Figure src={comfortFood} alt="Held food displaying its Comfort preview" caption="Food values are shown before you eat." plate />

Food starts with the signed value from [Comfort food data](/en/wiki/comfort-food/). Well-made quality is applied first: levels 1–3 multiply positive values by `1 + 0.33 × level` and divide negative values by that factor, truncating towards zero.

The last 6 foods that produced a nonzero value are remembered by item type. Count how often the item already appears in that history:

| Prior occurrences | Positive value retained |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 100% |
| 1 | 70% |
| 2 | 40% |
| 3 or more | 10% |

Positive repetition-adjusted values round down but retain at least 1 point. Negative values skip the repetition penalty. A value that becomes 0 after quality scaling does not change Comfort or enter the history. `contented_diner` multiplies repeated-meal retention by ×1.25, capped at 1.00; `fussy_diner` uses ×0.75. The first serving stays at 100%. Personality applies after repetition, then the final Comfort value clamps to 0–100.

## Damage and sleep

Positive finite damage produces a loss based on the damage amount and its fraction of maximum health. The base loss is clamped to 1–20 points **before fate modifiers**; 20 is not a universal final cap.

When the world wakes its sleepers, players who have slept long enough to reset time receive Comfort recovery. Base recovery is the smaller of 30 and 70% of missing Comfort, rounded down. At 20 Comfort this gives 30; at 65 it gives 24, before fate modifiers. `restful_sleeper` multiplies this recovery by ×1.25 and `fitful_sleeper` by ×0.75, rounded before personality scaling.

## Fate Balance interactions

These [Fate Balance](/en/wiki/fate-balance/) choices change gain, loss and the interval between natural evaluations:

| Fate | Recovery | Loss | Evaluation interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `optimistic_nature` (Sunward Nature) | ×1.20 | ×0.80 | ×1.25 |
| `pessimistic_nature` (Gloomward Nature) | ×0.80 | ×1.20 | ×0.80 |

Intervals are multiplied and rounded to whole ticks, with a minimum of 1 tick (0.05 seconds). Adjusted gains/losses round to integers; a nonzero change retains at least 1 point in its original direction. Direct setting of the value bypasses the gain/loss multipliers.

Appetite choices affect hunger capacity separately:

| Fate | Maximum hunger |
| --- | --- |
| No appetite choice | 20 |
| `hearty_appetite` (Bottomless Appetite) | 26 |
| `birdlike_appetite` (Birdlike Appetite) | 14 |

## Fate Balance meal tags

Palates belong to [Fate Balance](/en/wiki/fate-balance/). Edit item tags under `data/isekaiexpansion/tags/items/fate_balance/meals/`: `fresh_produce.json`, `bread.json`, `cooked_fish.json`, `carrots.json` and `spicy_foods.json`. They use Minecraft item-tag syntax. Optional external entries let absent foods be skipped; the spicy tag includes `expandeddelight:chili_pepper` when installed.

Quality adjusts the base food value first. For a positive base, the selected palate adds +4 for matching meals or subtracts 3 for meals outside a restrictive palate. A zero or negative base bypasses palate changes. Positive adjusted values then use repetition and personality; negative values skip repetition. The food tooltip follows the same calculation. An empty food family makes its palate choices unavailable.

## For datapack authors

Food values live in `data/<namespace>/comfort_level/*.json`. The [food format](/en/wiki/comfort-food/) documents all fields, validation and reload behaviour. `/ise dev dumpFoodComfort` generates starting values for registered foods.
