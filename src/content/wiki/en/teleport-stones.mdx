A teleport stone carries you to the destination set in your travel ledger: Remote Sky Garden, one of up to 16 anchors you have attuned, or a member of your party. Holding the stone for 60 ticks (3 seconds) starts the crossing, and the stone's grade decides what each crossing costs.

Remote Sky Garden is the default destination for a new ledger when the world hub is ready. Its permanent row stays visible under every filter, cannot be renamed or forgotten and uses none of the `16` anchor slots. Crossings use the hub’s safe arrival plane; the usual toll and channel duration still apply. You can select an attuned anchor or a companion instead. See [World spawn](/en/wiki/world-spawn/).

## The three stones

Every stone reads the same ledger. The ledger belongs to you rather than to a stone, so a new stone already knows every anchor you have attuned and a stone that crumbles takes nothing with it.

| Stone | Stack size | Toll per crossing | Kept after use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teleport Stone·Alpha | 16 | Crumbles and drains all Astral Energy | No |
| Teleport Stone·Beta | 1 | A quarter of your maximum Astral Energy | Yes |
| Teleport Stone·Gamma | 1 | Free | Yes |

A toll never refuses a crossing: with too little Astral Energy you pay what you have. Creative mode pays no toll. After a crossing, that grade of stone cools down for 100 ticks (5 seconds).

New characters start with a Teleport Stone·Beta; the first-spawn kit belongs to Isekai Core. Cartographers can offer all three stones through their [trades](/en/wiki/trading/).

## Controls

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Hold Use | Channels for 60 ticks (3 seconds), then crosses to your destination. |
| Sneak + Use on an anchor block | Attunes the anchor and adds it to your ledger. |
| Sneak + Use anywhere else | Opens the travel ledger. |
| Use with no reachable destination | Opens the travel ledger and says why instead of channelling. |

Releasing Use before the channel finishes cancels it. The stone's tooltip shows your current destination and the toll; hold Shift to see the controls.

## Attuning anchors

Two kinds of block can be attuned: the Respawn Altar, and the waystones added by the Fabric Waystones mod when it is installed.

The Respawn Altar sets your respawn point when used with an empty main hand. It does not periodically apply Mining Fatigue to nearby players.

- **The first anchor you attune** becomes your destination if none is set.
- **An anchor already in your ledger** is not added twice.
- **A full ledger** holds 16 anchors; forget one before attuning another.

Anchors stay in your ledger through death and across dimensions. A ledger anchor can sit in any dimension, and a crossing to it may change dimension.

## Choosing a destination

The travel ledger lists your anchors in the order you arranged them, then your party members, with online members first. Filters show everything, anchors only or companions only. Selecting a row opens its details: realm, position, distance when it is in your realm, and status.

- **Set as Destination** makes the selected row your stone's destination. Double-clicking a row sets it and closes the ledger.
- **Rename** gives an anchor your own name of up to 32 characters. Saving an empty name restores the block's name.
- **Move Up** and **Move Down** change the anchor's place in the list.
- **Forget Anchor** removes the anchor after a second click within 3 seconds.

The keyboard works too: Up and Down move through the list, Enter sets the destination and F2 renames.

## Crossing

When the channel completes, the crossing checks the destination before you leave:

- **An anchor whose block is gone** is struck from your ledger, and you stay where you are.
- **An anchor with no safe ground** on any of its four sides cannot be reached; the crossing lands beside the anchor, never on top of it.
- **A party member** must be online, alive, not spectating and still in your party. You arrive where they stand.

The toll and the cooldown apply only when a crossing actually starts.

## Interactions

| System | Effect on teleport stones |
| --- | --- |
| Astral Energy | Pays the Alpha and Beta tolls. |
| Party | Party members appear as companions. The party system belongs to the pack's party integration; without a party the companion list is empty. |
| Fabric Waystones | Its waystones are anchor blocks when the mod is present. |

## Where the data lives

Anchor blocks are the `isekaiexpansion:teleportable_blocks` block tag, so a data pack can add or remove anchor blocks by editing that tag. Your ledger is stored with your character.
