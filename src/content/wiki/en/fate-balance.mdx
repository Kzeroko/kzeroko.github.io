Fate Balance shapes your daily life with a starting budget of 10 points. Blessings spend points; burdens supply up to 6 more. Meals, sleep, personality and social traits share this budget.

## The budget

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Starting points | `10` by default; server setting `fateBalanceMaxPoints`, limited to `0–64` |
| Burden contribution limit | `6` |
| Remaining points | Starting points + burden gain − blessing cost |
| Confirmation | Remaining points at least `0`; availability, prerequisites, conflicts and exclusive groups must all pass |
| Empty profile | Allowed; points may remain unspent |

Opposing pairs cannot be selected together. All palate paths share one exclusive group, so choose at most one palate blessing or burden. A palate is unavailable if its food tag contains no items. Categories are General, Rest, Mental, Social, Dining and Special.

## Building a profile

Select a path to inspect its effect, then choose or release it. Remove a conflicting choice before adding another. Undo and redo change the draft; reset restores the profile captured when the editor opened. Presets replace the draft and still require confirmation.

The server evaluates the complete profile again when you seal it. An invalid or overspent draft cannot be confirmed. The confirmed profile persists and is copied on respawn.

## Meals and rest

Palates apply only when the food's quality-adjusted base Comfort is positive. A palate blessing adds +4 to matching meals. A palate burden subtracts 3 from meals outside its family; the result can become negative. Zero and already-negative base foods keep their values. These are Comfort changes with no consume action or timed buff.

Food families use item tags under `isekaiexpansion:fate_balance/meals/`:

| Palate pair | Tag suffix | Foods |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `garden_palate` / `garden_bound` | `fresh_produce` | fresh produce |
| `hearth_palate` / `hearth_bound` | `bread` | bread |
| `coastal_palate` / `coastal_bound` | `cooked_fish` | cooked fish |
| `root_palate` / `root_bound` | `carrots` | carrot dishes |
| `spice_palate` / `spice_bound` | `spicy_foods` | spicy foods |

After the palate adjustment, positive meals use the [Comfort](/en/wiki/comfort/) history of 6 foods. The ordinary retained fractions are 1.00, 0.70, 0.40 and 0.10 for 0, 1, 2 and at least 3 prior occurrences. Dining traits multiply retention only for repeated meals, capped at 1.00. Positive results round down with a minimum of 1; negative results skip repetition. Personality applies afterwards. Food tooltips use the same calculation.

Completed sleep restores the smaller of 30 and 70% of missing Comfort, rounded down, then applies the sleep trait and rounds again. Personality applies last and Comfort clamps to 0–100. Only players sleeping long enough to reset time receive this recovery when the world wakes its sleepers.

## Other interactions

Loot and eligible quest rewards must use IsekaiCore's grant event to receive the learning multiplier. Expansion's direct `isekaiexpansion:xp` quest reward and level potion bypass it. Combat experience is unchanged. Grants round to integers; small grants may not change.

Fate Balance changes only the daily loot cap. Home-rest effects manage the combat cap separately. Each modified cap is recalculated every second from the current level; removing its last modifier restores that cap's default once. Neither path clears experience already used. See [level progression](/en/wiki/leveling/).

Personality scales non-direct Comfort changes and the decay interval. Gains and losses round to integers while retaining a signed unit for a nonzero result; direct assignments bypass the scaling. Countenance changes apply only to factions whose current reputation is at or below −100. Eloquence and Charm remain separate dialogue attributes. Appetite changes hunger capacity without defining a healing multiplier.

## Where the definitions live

Choices and effects are registered by the mod. Add-ons use the [Fate Balance API](/en/dev/fate-balance-api/); data packs can edit meal tags and [Comfort food values](/en/wiki/comfort-food/).
