Gems add attributes, elemental damage or skills to equipment with compatible sockets. Each socket holds 1 gem. The item's socket profile sets its kinds and level requirements; a gem may also carry an equipment restriction.

## What each socket provides

| Kind | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| `ATTRIBUTE` | Random attributes from a Fusible Gem. Bundled line-count weights are 80:17:3 for 1, 2 or 3 lines. |
| `FIXED` | Predefined attribute bonuses, sometimes scaled by the gem's loot difficulty. |
| `ELEMENT` | Damage from a configured spell school on a combat trigger. |
| `SKILL` | A skill triggered by combat or manual equipment-skill activation. |

Random and fixed gems must fit the allowed stat domains and damage categories. Element gems must fit the school and damage categories; skill gems must fit the allowed skill list. All random lines must qualify. A random gem's “Applicable to” restriction must also match the equipment, including after insertion.

## Inserting a gem

Hold the gem on the cursor and left-click eligible equipment, then select the socket. The screen can open with only 1 eligible slot. A successful attempt consumes 1 gem; a failed attempt keeps it. Closing returns the remaining source stack.

| Bundled insertion rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Base chance | 85% |
| Maximum chance | 95% |
| Strain added on failure | 1 |
| Chance bonus per strain | +6 percentage points |
| Strain removed on success | 2, floor 0 |
| Maximum stored strain | 10 |
| Crack chance after failure | 20% |

Strain belongs to the equipment, shared by its sockets. Starting at 0 strain, the next chance is 85%, then 91% after 1 failure and 95% after 2 failures. More failures cannot exceed 95%. The 20% crack roll happens only after a failed insertion.

Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> on the equipment tooltip for strain, next chance, socket-state counts and curse details.

## Locks, cracks and extraction

A level lock lifts when the equipment meets the socket's required [equipment level](/en/wiki/leveling/). A seal is a separate persistent lock that Artificer's Lens can toggle on an empty, level-unlocked socket.

Locked and cracked sockets cannot accept gems or provide gem attributes/procs. A cracked socket keeps any gem already inside. Cracks allow extraction; locks block it. Repair the crack with Kintsugi Resin before inserting again.

| Extraction tool | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Gem Cutter | Selected gem has a 50% chance to shatter. |
| Mythril Gem Cutter | Safe extraction. |
| Void Chisel | Consumable extraction with 50% shatter chance. |
| Aetherial Calipers | Consumable safe extraction. |

Cutters lose durability on use outside creative mode. Extraction leaves the socket's saved cooldown in place.

## Consumables

Inapplicable consumables do nothing and are not spent. Bundled consumables use a certain action roll; Void Chisel's separate shatter roll still applies.

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Chaos Prism | Replaces random lines, values and equipment restriction. |
| Perfection Dust | Keeps attributes, operations and restriction; rerolls values. |
| Entropic Dodecahedron | Rerolls the selected socketed random gem, including its restriction. |
| Void Chisel | Extracts a gem with 50% shatter chance. |
| Aetherial Calipers | Extracts safely. |
| Artificer's Lens | Toggles the seal on an empty socket after its level unlock. |
| Mutating Clay | Changes an empty, unlocked, uncracked socket to another kind. |
| Blood-Pact Quill | Binds a random configured curse to uncursed equipment. |
| Hallowed Ash | Removes the bound curse. |
| Kintsugi Resin | Repairs a cracked socket after its level unlock; sealing does not block repair. |
| Harmonic Oil | Removes 1 strain, to a minimum of 0. |

Rerolls keep the stored difficulty context and reapply its scaling from unscaled base values. Replacing lines can also change the gem's equipment restriction. Reducing strain with Harmonic Oil reduces the insertion bonus that strain supplied.

## Combat and manual skills

| Trigger | Equipment scanned |
| --- | --- |
| `MELEE_HIT`, `RANGED_HIT`, `MAGIC_HIT` | Attacker's hands for the matching damage category. |
| `ON_KILL` | Killer's hands. |
| `ON_HURT` | Defender's armour and both hands. |
| `TRIGGERABLE` | Manual skill search: main hand, offhand, head, chest, legs, feet. |

Combat proc scans do not include trinkets. Skill gems require a player and can cost astral energy. Each socket has its own cooldown. Element gems cannot use `TRIGGERABLE`.

Manual activation is deterministic once its cooldown and energy checks pass. It selects the first compatible gem in the listed order, then the first socket. If that gem is cooling down or unaffordable, it does not skip ahead. See [Equipment skills](/en/wiki/equipment-skills/) for activation controls.

## Curses

Equipment can carry 1 bound curse. Its stored strength scales the curse's attribute penalties, status duration or incoming-damage bonus. Curse status and damage effects collect from armour, both hands and trinkets; their scan is broader than combat gem procs. Hallowed Ash removes the bound curse.

## Bundled socket progression

The first matching profile wins: higher priority first, then profile ID. This table shows the bundled profiles, so custom packs and overlapping item tags can change the result.

| Equipment group | Kind and minimum equipment level |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible relic/trinket tags | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0; the profile excludes parachutes, Create goggles, Backpacked backpacks and lanterns. |
| Armour | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0, `FIXED` at 10. |
| Shields | `FIXED` at 0, `ATTRIBUTE` at 10. |
| IsekaiCore shields | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0 and 10. |
| Melee weapons | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0 and 10, melee `ELEMENT` at 20. |
| Ranged weapons | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0 and 10, ranged `ELEMENT` at 20. |
| Wands and staves | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0, magic `ELEMENT` at 10, `SKILL` at 20. |
| Tools | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0. |
| `isekaiexpansion:origami_amanoyozakura` | `ATTRIBUTE` at 0, `SKILL` at 20. |

## Where the data lives

The rules come from `gem_system`, `gem_attributes`, `socket_profiles`, `curses` and `gem_consumables` data resources. [Gem data](/en/wiki/gem-data/) documents every field, conditional compatibility, validation and reload behaviour.
