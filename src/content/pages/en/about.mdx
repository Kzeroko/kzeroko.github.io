I am Kzeroko, and I build Minecraft RPG modpacks on my own.

The one I am working on is **KTM2** — KenToMahou 2: The Chronicles of Urdas. It runs on 1.20.1 with the Fabric mod loader, ships 268 mods, and carries a full set of modules I wrote myself, the [Isekai Series](/en/ktm2/modules/). The world-building, the systems, the Java, the structures, the textures and the localisation are all basically mine.

The continent is original, but it is not a fixed map. Terrain distribution comes from ReTerraForged, and towns, dungeons and ruins are still generated, so no two saves look the same.

## What this site is

Four things under one domain:

- **[KTM2](/en/ktm2/)** — the official page for the modpack and the in-house modules.
- **[Wiki](/en/wiki/)** — player-facing system documentation. Numbers are checked against the shipped data files, and every page carries the date it was verified.
- **[Codex](/en/codex/)** — the canon of Urdas, spoiler-free layer only.
- **[Devlog](/en/blog/)** — where things are, and why I did them that way.

## Where things get published

Everything about KTM2 is published **here first**, then on Bilibili.

Anything you find on another platform is a reupload I had no part in. If you see a download link somewhere else with my name on it, I did not put it there.
